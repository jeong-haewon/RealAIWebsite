Accelerating Scientific Discovery Through AI

Your support enables us to push the boundaries of AI-powered research, from understanding the human brain to modeling complex biological systems. Contributions to REAL AI directly fund cutting-edge research, train the next generation of scientists, and help us build AI-powered digital twins of complex systems—from the brain to the pancreas to the universe. Together, we can transform how science is done and make discoveries that benefit humanity.
